01 · Philosophy

Massage as quiet ceremony.

A practice rooted in slow listening — not technique alone.
Samādhi — समाधि — the Sanskrit word for the deep, unbroken absorption that arrives when the body finally stops bracing.

Each session is shaped around the body in front of me. I work without rush — long, considered strokes that follow the breath rather than a timer.

You arrive with whatever you're carrying. We leave a little more of it on the table.

— a practice of presence, not performance.

— · What I work with

Where the body asks for attention.

Every session adapts to what you bring in

Full body

A complete, unhurried session to release held tension, ease circulation, and return mobility after long days, physical effort, or weeks lived in a hurry. Shaped to the body in front of me.

Legs & feet

Focused recovery for tired legs and sore feet — reducing muscle fatigue, lifting circulation, and helping the body land more softly into the next day. Especially useful after travel or long walks.

Back & shoulders

Targeted work for back, neck, and shoulder tension carried from posture, screens, or daily load. Designed to soften stiffness and let the upper body breathe and move freely again.

04 · About

Hello — I'm Victor.

A practitioner first, a host second.

I've been practicing bodywork for over a decade — first in Brazil, where I trained, then in studios across South America before settling here. My background blends Swedish and deep-tissue technique with the slower, breath-led traditions I've returned to over the years.

What I care about most: that the hour belongs to you. No rushed intake, no upselling, no pressure to do more than you came for. Just careful, attentive work — and the time to actually feel it land.
